## Design Context

### Users
One user: the maker, a serious home brewer (Certified Cicerone candidate) who designs recipes,
clones medal winners, names beers, and monitors fermentations with Tilt/iSpindel hydrometers and
Home Assistant temperature control. The app runs on a Raspberry Pi; there is no wall display.

Contexts (all three matter):
- **Phone** — quick checks on active batches, alerts, gravity/temperature, from anywhere.
- **Tablet** — brew-day companion near the kettle and fermenters: steps, timers, readings, the assistant.
- **Desk computer** — dense work: recipe design, water chemistry, inventory, batch history, the AI assistant.

Jobs: track active fermentations (gravity, temperature, attenuation, ABV); design and refine recipes
(style fit, IBU/SRM/OG, hop schedule); manage inventory (hops, yeast, equipment); log brew days and
tasting notes; ask the AI brewing assistant for reviews and changes.

### Brand Personality
Craft and rigor together: a brewer's tool made with care, not a generic SaaS dashboard.
Numbers are instruments (gravity, temperature, ABV must read precisely and at a glance).
The test: someone asks "how was this made?", never "which AI made this?".

### Aesthetic Direction
- Theme: **dark default** (evenings, garage, phone at night) with a **light variant** for desk work;
  same visual vocabulary in both.
- Direction itself: open. Fresh mockups (2026-11-11) to choose from; the old warm-industrial
  "Analog Brewhouse" is one candidate among them.
- Anti-references: generic zinc/slate dark dashboard; Geist + Crimson Pro (what the app uses today);
  glassmorphism/glow; border-left accent stripes; gradient text; equal cookie-cutter card grids;
  cyan/purple "AI" palettes; modals for everything.

### Accessibility
No special needs beyond good practice: WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, honour
`prefers-reduced-motion`, comfortable touch targets on phone/tablet.

### Design Principles
1. **Numbers are instruments** — tabular numerals, clear units, sized for the glance on phone and tablet.
2. **One app, three screens** — phone (glance), tablet (brew day), desk (dense work); adapt layouts, never amputate features.
3. **Craft over template** — every screen should feel made for brewing; no generic dashboard furniture.
4. **Accent is rare** — one signature accent colour, neutrals tinted toward the brand hue.
5. **Quiet until it matters** — calm by default; alerts, stuck fermentations and approvals get the emphasis.
